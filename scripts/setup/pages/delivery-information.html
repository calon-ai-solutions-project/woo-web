Pallet delivery

Pallets and some large lots are delivered by a pallet network. Because the cost depends on the delivery address and access, pallet delivery is quoted after you order.

  1. You place and pay for your order. Delivery for the pallet is shown as quoted after your order.
  2. We email you a delivery quote within [QUOTE TIME].
  3. Once you approve and pay the quote, we book the delivery and send you the date.
  4. If you do not accept the quote, you can cancel the pallet and we refund what you paid for it in full.

Pallets are delivered to the kerbside by a vehicle with a tail lift [CHECK WITH YOUR PALLET NETWORK]. Please make sure there is safe access for a lorry and a pallet truck, and someone available to receive the delivery. Tell us about any access limits, such as narrow roads, steps or no hard standing, when you order. If a delivery cannot be made because of access we did not know about, the pallet company may charge for a second attempt.

Orders paid by bank transfer

If you pay by bank transfer, we send your order once the payment has cleared in our account. Please use your order number as the payment reference.

When your order arrives

Please check your delivery when it arrives. If the packaging is badly damaged, note this when you sign for it, or refuse a pallet that is visibly damaged. Tell us about missing or damaged items within [DAMAGE REPORT TIME] of delivery, with photos if you can, so we can claim from the courier and put it right.
