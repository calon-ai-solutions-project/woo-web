This page explains how to cancel an order or return an item. The rules are different for consumers and for business buyers, so please read the section that applies to you.

You are a consumer if you buy for your own personal use. You are a business buyer if you buy for resale or for use in a trade, business or profession. Wholesale, job lot and pallet orders are normally business purchases.

Consumers: your 14-day right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations you can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days. The 14 days start the day after you receive the goods. If your order comes in more than one delivery, they start the day after the last delivery.

  1. Tell us you want to cancel before the 14 days end. Use our contact form or email [CONTACT EMAIL] with your order number. You can use the model cancellation form at the end of this page, but you do not have to.
  2. Send the goods back to [RETURNS ADDRESS] within 14 days of telling us.
  3. We refund you within 14 days of receiving the goods back, or of you showing us proof that you have sent them, whichever is sooner. The refund includes the standard delivery charge you paid, and goes back to the way you paid.

You pay the cost of sending goods back, unless they are faulty or not as described. [CONFIRM WHO PAYS FOR RETURNS. IF THE OWNER WILL PAY FOR RETURNS, CHANGE THIS SENTENCE.] Please pack items carefully and get proof of postage. You can handle the goods as you would in a shop, but if they are returned used or damaged beyond that, we may reduce your refund to reflect the loss in value.

The right to cancel does not apply to sealed goods that are not suitable for return for health or hygiene reasons once unsealed, such as cosmetics, toiletries and personal care items you have opened.

Consumers: faulty goods

Under the Consumer Rights Act, goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. Many of our products are clearance stock, customer returns or used items, and each listing shows a condition grade and describes any known faults. Faults we described before you bought are not grounds for a refund, but your rights for any other fault stay the same.

  • Within 30 days of delivery you can reject a faulty item and get a full refund.
  • After 30 days, we will offer a repair or replacement. If that is not possible or does not fix the problem, you can have a price reduction or a refund. If the item was delivered more than six months ago, a refund may be reduced to reflect the use you have had.
  • If the item is faulty, we pay the reasonable cost of returning it.

Please contact us before sending anything back, with your order number and a description or photos of the fault, so we can tell you the quickest way to put it right.

Business buyers

The 14-day right to cancel is a consumer right and does not apply to business purchases. Wholesale stock, job lots and pallets are sold on these terms:

  • We do not accept returns because you have changed your mind.
  • Customer returns sold as untested are sold as seen. We do not accept returns of untested stock because some items do not work.
  • Item counts and descriptions for job lots and pallets are approximate. A small difference in the number or mix of items is not a reason for a refund. [STATE THE TOLERANCE, FOR EXAMPLE PLUS OR MINUS 5 PERCENT.]
  • If goods arrive damaged in transit, or are materially different from the listing, tell us within [BUSINESS CLAIM TIME] of delivery, with photos. Where we agree, we will replace the goods, give a credit or give a refund.
  • Goods must not be resold, opened or broken down before a claim has been agreed with us.

Nothing on this page limits any right a business buyer has by law that cannot be excluded.
