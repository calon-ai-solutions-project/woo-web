Stock for businesses, resellers, market traders and anyone buying in quantity, from a job lot to a full pallet.

Two ways to buy in bulk

  • Lots and pallets. Job lots and pallets are listed as single products, with the approximate number of items and where the stock came from.
  • Bulk prices. Many products cost less each when you buy more. The quantity prices are shown on the product page and applied in your basket automatically.

Good to know

  • Some bulk listings have a minimum order quantity. It is shown on the product page.
  • You can pay by bank transfer at checkout. We send the order once the payment has cleared.
  • Pallet delivery is quoted after you order. We email you the cost to approve before we send it.
  • Every listing shows a condition grade. Customer returns marked untested are sold as seen.
